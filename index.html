About Me

I am an undergraduate student at Shandong University and a research assistant at HKUST (GZ), advised by Prof. Yutao Yue. I will join the Ph.D. program in Artificial Intelligence at HKUST (GZ) in Fall 2027.

My research focuses on embodied intelligence and robot learning, bridging high-level behavior generation and decision-making with reliable robot control. My interests span vision-language-action models, world-action models, embodied agents, and reinforcement-learning-based motion tracking and whole-body control, with an emphasis on closed-loop execution, long-horizon tasks, and sim-to-real transfer.
